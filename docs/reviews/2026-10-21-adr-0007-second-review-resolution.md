# ADR-0007 final amendment resolution

Date: 2026-10-21  
Subject: [ADR-0007](../adr/0007-event-driven-judge-attention-and-compact-evidence.md)  
Source: [second review](2026-10-21-adr-0007-second-review.md), the user's final amendment proposal and instruction to incorporate the assessed changes  
Disposition: Documentation amended. ADR-0007 remains Proposed pending a separate architecture acceptance decision. No session registration/transfer, PoC, model invocation or mode enablement is performed by this change.

The [first resolution](2026-10-21-adr-0007-review-resolution.md) and both reviews remain historical records. The amendments refine the contracts below rather than reopen unrelated ADR7-R01–R05 findings. In particular, the old mandatory 50% attention rule is superseded, the delivery deadline now requires actual turn start, ownership guarantees depend on the registered configuration, and fixed projection slice size is no longer normative. The reproducible baseline and all five evidence/verifier files remain byte-identical.

| Proposal / review point | Final contract | Existing criteria refined |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ownership modes; ADR7-S01 | Support Dedicated Judge Session, preferred for the first implementation, and Interactive Judge Session. Dedicated ownership excludes concurrent writers across supported clients/processes and requires explicit fenced takeover; interactive ownership additionally requires atomic exclusion of user turns/composition. No silent replacement of this chat. Keep the ADR-0006 worker, scoped tools, managed-invocation refusal and shared budgets. | ADR7-02, ADR7-14, ADR7-15 |
| Delivery and quiesce; ADR7-S02 | Separate persisted request, adapter receipt, admission, actual start, reply receipt and applied decision. Busy is a deferral cause, not successful delivery or transport failure. Confirmed `turn_started` satisfies the 30 s deadline; committed `decision_applied` satisfies the 900 s deadline. Both remain anchored to request creation. Stop new dispatch; settle in-flight work under captured finish/terminate policy without a new grace period. Terminal runs remain terminal. | ADR7-02, ADR7-09, ADR7-16 |
| Stagnation versus cost; ADR7-S03 | Preserve deterministic repeated-failure, unchanged-product and repeated-action detectors. Move the 50% allowance crossing to a separate `budget_checkpoint`, diagnostic/continue by default. An explicit saved policy may escalate it as a budget event. Two productive unfinished iterations may reach the third without Judge inference. Hard limits and existing barriers remain enforced. | ADR7-12, ADR7-13 |
| Competing requests | Maintain independent named-request barriers, priority and explicit causal supersession. Late `CONTINUE` or `ESCALATE` resolution cannot override higher-priority Stop/revocation or unrelated barriers. Validate current owner generation, subject/revision, authority and budgets when applying a decision/action. | ADR7-08, ADR7-14 |
| Unknown delivery | Transport redelivery is distinct from model initiation. After a lost `turn/start` acknowledgement, including actual start before connection loss, reconcile external request/turn identity or retain unknown state/reservation for intervention. Do not promise exactly-once invocation without a verified client guarantee. | ADR7-07, ADR7-15 |
| Evidence trust | Keep source, streams, reports, paths and artifacts untrusted; distinguish engine facts, model claims and external content. Enforce decision/operation authority independently. Inert rendering and structured packaging are not complete prompt-injection prevention. Preserve provenance, integrity, redaction and full audit. | ADR7-08, ADR7-17 |
| Projection and timing | Retain incremental projection, 1 s critical visibility/package readiness, 5 s adapter receipt and at-most-100 ms measured event-loop delay in predefined supported-load scenarios. Measure actual Judge start separately. A 10 ms slice, worker threads or a native-language implementation is not required. Do not wait for full coverage before sending an alarm; incomplete coverage cannot establish acceptance. | ADR7-05, ADR7-06 |
| Feasibility and scope; ADR7-S04 | Before the main implementation, conduct an authorized bounded PoC for exact-session resume, scoped MCP tools, ownership/exclusion, crash/unknown-start recovery and accounting on the current Codex version. Stage 1 publishes separate versioned request/delivery/decision/timing contracts. Keep the same 17 acceptance IDs and unchanged baseline. Use existing Engine, Bridge, Outbox and ADR-0006 worker only. | ADR7-02, ADR7-07, ADR7-11, ADR7-14, ADR7-15 |

Architecture may be accepted by a recorded user decision before implementation evidence exists. Enablement of a particular ownership/client configuration still requires the PoC and applicable integration acceptance tests. A verified dedicated configuration does not certify interactive use, or vice versa. ADR-0002 through ADR-0006 authority, budget, Stop, lifecycle and quality boundaries remain in force; no periodic Judge turns or new scheduler/monitoring agent/service are introduced.
