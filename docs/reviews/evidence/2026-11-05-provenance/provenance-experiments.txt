Conclusion: identical inputs give bytes that differ only in the linker timestamp fields and the CodeView GUID (legitimate nondeterminism of MSVC linking without /Brepro). The shim compiled by 'tauri build' (which passes --features tauri/custom-protocol) differs by about 25 kB of code and data from the standalone 'shim:build' compilation. Both were published to different paths before this change: the runtime received the standalone copy, the receipt bound that copy, and src-tauri/target/release held the tauri copy. That is a two-producer defect, not nondeterminism. Single-producer fix: build-finish publishes the tauri-compiled copy and verifies it.
